# HEETF Series Closure — v0.1.0

## 異質存在、體驗域與技術適配研究系列
### Heterogeneous Existence, Experiential Domains, and Technological Fit Series

**Series ID:** HEETF  
**Closure date:** 2026-08-15  
**Closed papers:** HEETF-01 through HEETF-08

## Series Arc

1. **HEETF-01 — 科技進步不等於人生進步**
   Capability progress does not entail subject-indexed welfare progress.

2. **HEETF-02 — 主體域與體驗域**
   Shared world does not entail shared lived world.

3. **HEETF-03 — 結果—體驗非等價**
   Same outcome does not entail same path, experience, or lived value.

4. **HEETF-04 — 異質效益斷層**
   Same task may have radically different typed cost functions.

5. **HEETF-05 — 載體上限**
   Same semantic task may pass through different carrier bottleneck topologies.

6. **HEETF-06 — 比較域失效**
   There is no natural universal leaderboard without a declared comparison domain.

7. **HEETF-07 — 選擇自己的技術時代**
   Civilizational maximum technology need not be the personal experiential optimum.

8. **HEETF-08 — 異質存在的共同世界**
   Heterogeneous local worlds can coexist through translation, protocols, verification, rights floors, and shared-substrate governance.

## Closure Rule

HEETF-01 through HEETF-08 are treated as the complete first series.

Do not create HEETF-09 as an automatic continuation. Future work should either:
- open a clearly named HEETF Series II; or
- branch into a new dedicated series (e.g. human–AI rights, cross-world governance, translation protocols, memory-gated virtual worlds).

## Anti-Overclaim Summary

The series does **not** prove:
- all subjects are phenomenally different;
- AI has or lacks phenomenal consciousness;
- technology usually decreases welfare;
- human and AI efficiency has one universal scalar;
- current VR supports full lifetime alternate worlds;
- current neuroscience supports precise concept-level memory sealing;
- any proposed HEETF right is already a current legal right;
- all differences can be translated;
- pluralism eliminates conflict.

It establishes conditional distinctions, typed comparison rules, formal non-implications, and coexistence architectures.
