Formal boundary notes:
- The Persistent Contact Theorem is proved directly in this paper by a conditional-hazard product bound and does not depend on independence.
- The finite-sum counterexample is stated under an independence model.
- The Prominence Pressure Conjecture is not claimed as an established theorem of social diffusion.
- Existing cumulative-advantage, preferential-attachment, attention-competition and cascade literature provides adjacent mechanisms and counterweights, not a direct proof of the conjecture.
- The effective information mass, channel entropy, prominence-hazard formulation, and PPSS-PPC cohort are proposed constructs.
