# SOURCE_POLICY

`paper.md` is the canonical UTF-8 source artifact.

1. Mathematical source uses only `$...$` and `$$...$$`.
2. No Unicode-escape round trip is used.
3. LaTeX commands are preserved as source text.
4. Validation precedes checksum generation and release packaging.
5. Current 2025–2026 literature on human enhancement, the right to remain unaugmented, mind uploading/personal identity, brain emulation, human extinction ethics, and posthumanism was freshly searched before drafting.
6. Internal dependencies include CIND-01—06, 後人類奇點前夜, 後人類匯流, 誰仍然是人, 歷史構成與數位身份連續性, 跨主體普世主義, PGMV, and UFI.
7. Human extinction, posthuman transition, mind uploading, and artificial subjecthood are not predicted or asserted as inevitable.
8. Enhancement freedom and non-enhancement freedom are both preserved.
9. Functional continuity is explicitly separated from subject continuity.
