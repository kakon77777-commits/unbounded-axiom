# JDPSP Series I — Closure Note

Series I is closed at 9 papers.

1. JDPSP-01 — 概率論的歷史、學派與前沿分歧
2. JDPSP-02 — 判定域概率論：概率之前的容器、空間與量詞

4. JDPSP-04 — 多尺度概率幾何：微觀、中觀、宏觀與全域判定空間
5. JDPSP-05 — 遞歸概率場：概率之上的概率、階數保持與尺度交叉
6. JDPSP-06 — 零測度支撐點與概率空間的構成元
7. JDPSP-07 — 動態判定域與開放系統概率
8. JDPSP-08 — AI 多尺度概率場：從 Token、語義與策略到任務能力的可重現分布
9. JDPSP-09 — 超概率統一框架：判定域、尺度、時間、遞歸階與傳輸見證的公理化

## Formal English framework name

Unified Judgment-Domain Probability Framework (UJDPF)

“Superprobability / 超概率” is retained only as the Chinese project-level umbrella.
It is not used as the formal English mathematical name because “superprobability”
already appears in supersymmetric probability/statistical-mechanics literature.
