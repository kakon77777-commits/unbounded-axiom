Boundary notes:
- EOID is the unifying framework introduced in this paper.
- The Layer Non-Determinacy Theorem is a structural theorem about environment-dependent transition kernels.
- The Necessary-Stage Bottleneck Bound follows directly from event-set inclusion.
- The canonical eight-layer chain is a standard ordering, not a claim that every object must follow one rigid path.
- EOID does not claim originality for retrievability, collective attention, social influence, structural virality, Hawkes processes, Bass diffusion, WOM effects, or outcome-quality dips.
- The contribution is cross-layer unification, bottleneck reasoning, entity-artifact coupling, and a shared state/transition vocabulary.
