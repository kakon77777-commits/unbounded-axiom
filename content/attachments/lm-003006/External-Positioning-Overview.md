# 全系列外部定位參考資料總覽

本系列每篇寫作前均重新進行外部定位檢索。核心外部接口包括：

- Claude E. Shannon, A Mathematical Theory of Communication (1948)
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Types and Tokens
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Identity
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Relative Identity
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Identity Over Time
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Personal Identity
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Names
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Reference
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Rigid Designators
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Social Ontology
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Speech Acts
- W3C PROV
- W3C namespace / RDF identifier models

用途：外部定位、術語邊界與相鄰研究比對；MIS/SID 的公式、certificate、operator 與 single-symbol identity framework 為本系列自行建立的工作框架。
