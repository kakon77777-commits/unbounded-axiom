8. Preference-heterogeneity/platform-adoption literature is used as adjacent support for heterogeneous consumer demand and is not treated as establishing AMNESS audience-capacity constructs.

Boundary notes:
- Effective Audience Capacity, Hard Addressable Audience, Quality-to-Capacity Elasticity, Niche-Locked Excellence, Audience Expansion Operator, Massification Cost, Discoverability-vs-Audience-Ceiling diagnosis, and AMNESS-AC are proposed constructs in this paper.
- Audience Capacity Bound and Mass-Success Impossibility Corollary are direct consequences of user-level adoption-probability bounds under the stated unique-adoption model.
- The Niche-Lock Ceiling Theorem is conditional on the addressable audience set remaining fixed above Q0.
- The Long-Tail Aggregate Value construction is a set-theoretic existence result, not an empirical claim that niche audiences are disjoint in real markets.
- Long-tail literature supports the empirical importance of niche products and lower search costs; the paper does not claim that long tails eliminate superstar concentration.
