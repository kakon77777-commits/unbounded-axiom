## Audit principle

G4 phase terminology survives as a scientific/computational mechanism only when:
1. its type is explicit;
2. its operational implementation exists;
3. its extra structure survives ablation against a non-phase typed baseline;
4. costs, errors, coverage, and uncertainty are reported.
