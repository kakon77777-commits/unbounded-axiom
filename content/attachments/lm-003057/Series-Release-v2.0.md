# LSI-PSD Expanded v2.0 — Complete Series

This package contains the twelve expanded papers of the
**Logic-Space Integration and Proof-Space Dynamics** series.

## Contents

- `papers/` — 12 canonical UTF-8 Markdown manuscripts.
- `individual_releases/` — the 12 individually validated ZIP releases.
- `LSI_PSD_Expanded_v2.0_Complete_Series.md` — concatenated canonical reading copy.
- `MANIFEST.json` — sizes, line counts, and hashes.
- `CHECKSUMS.sha256` — hashes for all top-level and paper files.

The individual releases retain their own validation reports and checksums.
