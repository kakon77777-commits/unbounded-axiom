# SOURCES — Paper 01

## External philosophical anchors

- Clark & Chalmers (1998), The Extended Mind: challenges the assumption that all cognitive processes must remain inside the biological organism.
- Andy Clark, Supersizing the Mind: develops embodied, situated, distributed and extended cognition.
- Varela, Thompson & Rosch, The Embodied Mind: treats cognition as embodied and enacted through organism–environment interaction rather than as merely detached internal representation.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Personal Identity: surveys persistence, characterization and competing accounts of personal identity.

## Paper 01 deliberate constraints

This series is written in philosophical-ontological prose.

- No mathematical formalization.
- No equations.
- No numerical scoring.
- Logical causality must remain explicit.
- Subject-domain language must not collapse into relativism, mysticism, or an unrestricted “everything connected to me is me” claim.
