## Main result

An arbitrary representation

$$
\phi:D\to X
$$

induces the concrete fiber closure

$$
\operatorname{Sat}_\phi(A)=\phi^{-1}(\phi(A)).
$$

A concrete proof-relevant set is exactly representable in the route domain iff it is closed under this fiber saturation. Therefore proof-safe compression does not require injectivity; it requires either proof-relevant fiber homogeneity or conservative retention plus whole-fiber exclusion certificates.
