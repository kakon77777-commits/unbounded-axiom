Boundary notes:
- AMNESS and the formal Quality Non-Determinacy Theorem are introduced in this paper.
- The theorem is structural: it states that if success genuinely depends on non-quality variables, quality alone cannot universally determine success.
- The rank-reversal example is constructive, not an empirical law that low-quality items usually outperform high-quality items.
- Existing cultural-market, recommendation, cold-start, communication, and diffusion research provides empirical mechanisms consistent with the separation of quality, exposure, popularity, and success.
- The paper does not claim a universal scalar measure of quality.
