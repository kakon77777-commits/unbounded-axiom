# Source Map — Paper 06

## External academic anchors

- Jiang, Xi & Xie (2024): influential retired leaders can constrain incumbent autocrats through informal political clout, especially personnel networks; the effect is distinct from formal office.
- Higashijima & Shiraito (2026): tutelary power by a former autocrat does not automatically raise a successor's public support; guardianship is not universally legitimacy-enhancing.
- Oliver (2024): former democratic presidents can retain substantial rhetorical and cue-giving influence without formal governing authority, supporting the distinction between influence and sovereignty.
- Boix & Svolik (2013): effective power sharing requires real constraints, not nominal institutions.
- Ji (2026): retention of a critical military office can preserve strong informal influence even after other top portfolios are relinquished.
- Svolik (2012) and Baturo (2014): succession, power concentration, and tenure are central to authoritarian political risk.
