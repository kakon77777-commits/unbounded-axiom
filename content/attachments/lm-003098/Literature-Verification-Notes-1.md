Boundary notes:
- Review Propensity Field, Review Multiplier Surface, Review–Adoption Non-Identifiability Theorem, Proxy Transportability, Popularity–Sales Gap, AMNESS-WAD, and related diagnostic constructs are proposed in this paper.
- The No Universal Review Multiplier Theorem is a direct structural result under E[R]=rho*A.
- Review-to-sales empirical effects are not claimed to have one universal sign or magnitude; meta-analytic literature explicitly reports heterogeneity by platform, product, and metric.
- Reviews may be both causes and outcomes of sales/adoption; dynamic simultaneity is therefore a central modeling assumption, not a fixed one-way causal claim.
