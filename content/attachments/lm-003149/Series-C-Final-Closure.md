## 3. 系列最終立場

本系列不主張：
- multi-agent consensus 等於 truth；
- 計算機等於完備 oracle；
- 現有 Agent 已等於 AGI；
- AI work society 等於人類社會；
- capability growth 必然降低安全；
- formal world 永遠比 physical / open world 更可靠。

本系列支持的較弱結論是：

$$
\boxed{
\text{A heterogeneous intelligent system can become increasingly
self-correcting and autonomous when it has sufficiently diverse
observers, reliable epistemic carriers, persistent state,
evidence-sensitive policy revision, organizational coordination,
bounded authority, and dense verification channels.}
}
$$
