Boundary note:
- The paper's "Machine-First Recognition Regime" and "Latent Machine Recognizability" are proposed theoretical constructs.
- Existing literature supports the separability of search exposure, long-tail machine resolution, retrieval-based entity knowledge, and user attention, but does not by itself establish the full PPSS decoupling model.
