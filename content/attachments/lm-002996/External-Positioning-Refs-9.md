# 外部定位參考資料

檢索日期：2026-08-13。

1. Emil L. Post, *The Two-Valued Iterative Systems of Mathematical Logic*, Princeton University Press, 1941.
   - complete systems、independent generators、iterative composition；本文僅作「生成基底／完備性」形式類比，不轉移其 Boolean theorem。

2. Andrzej Wiśniewski, *The Posing of Questions: Logical Foundations of Erotetic Inferences*, 1995.
   - question generation、erotetic inference。

3. Andrzej Wiśniewski, “Deduction and Reduction Theorems for Inferential Erotetic Logic,” *Studia Logica* 106 (2018), 295–309.
   - question evocation、erotetic implication。

4. Zhaohua Luo, “Clone Theory: Its Syntax and Semantics, Applications to Universal Algebra, Lambda Calculus and Algebraic Logic,” arXiv:0810.3162.
   - syntax represented by algebraic structures closed under composition；本文只列作未來 composition-closure 研究接口。

## 邊界

- Benchmark coverage 不等於 completeness proof。
- Ablation loss 不等於 generator independence proof。
- Speculative Semantic Execution 是本文工作術語，不等同 CPU speculative execution。
- Convergent Re-linking（收連）是系列內 provisional definition，不宣稱為既有標準術語。
