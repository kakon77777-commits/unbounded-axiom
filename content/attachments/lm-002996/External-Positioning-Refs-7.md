2. Andrzej Wiśniewski, *The Posing of Questions: Logical Foundations of Erotetic Inferences*, 1995.

   - formal questions/dependencies、normal-form/completeness interfaces。

5. Ivano Ciardelli, “Inquisitive Neighborhood Logic,” arXiv:2411.04031.
   - modal/conditional operators applicable to statements and questions。
