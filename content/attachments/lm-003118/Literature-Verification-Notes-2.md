Boundary notes:
- Observer-Dependent Perceived Prominence (ODPP), the observer rank-reversal theorem in PPSS, Search-Abundance Prominence Illusion, and the proposed ODPP Benchmark are theoretical constructs introduced in this paper.
- False-fame and source-monitoring literature supports the claim that fame judgments can be influenced by familiarity and source attribution, but does not directly validate the full online/search/AI model.
- Expertise literature supports observer heterogeneity and calibration differences, not a universal rule that experts always estimate fame better.
