# 1. Grounding and First-Cause Reconciliation

## 1.1 Grounding does not collapse into causation or ontological dependence

Rydéhn's work on grounding and ontological dependence argues that, on a standard factive conception of grounding, no known rigid ontological-dependence relation is generally either necessary or sufficient for grounding without substantive bridge assumptions.

UCT should therefore preserve:

$$
\\mathsf{Grounding}
\\neq
\\mathsf{Causation}
\\neq
\\mathsf{OntologicalDependence}.
$$

A grounding-to-dependence inference is a typed bridge problem.

## 1.2 A first-cause argument need not be a first-event argument

Pearce's *Foundational Grounding and the Argument from Contingency* is strong prior art for a move already central to Paper 02: the total causal History should not be explained by simply prepending another causal event to History. Pearce instead proposes a non-causal foundational ground.

This means the following distinction is **not** by itself novel:

$$
\\mathsf{CausalPriority}
\\neq
\\mathsf{FoundationalGround}.
$$

It remains important as a canonical invariant.

## 1.3 Grounding completeness is not regress termination

Leuenberger distinguishes two conceptions of fundamentality:

- **B-fundamentality:** being ungrounded;
- **A-fundamentality:** belonging to every grounding base / participating indispensably in a complete grounding basis.

Oberle further shows that completeness-based fundamentality can be made compatible with self-grounding or mutual grounding if strict-order assumptions on grounding are relaxed.

Therefore the current scalar notion:

$$
\\mathsf{GroundingComplete}
$$

is too coarse.

### Canonical repair

Replace it by:

$$
\\mathsf{GroundStatus}(S;D,\\Theta)
=
\\left\\langle
\\mathsf{CoverageComplete},
\\mathsf{RegressStatus},
\\mathsf{WellFoundedness},
\\mathsf{IndependenceStatus},
\\mathsf{GroundingBaseStatus}
\\right\\rangle.
$$

Recommended `RegressStatus` values:

- `terminated`
- `self_grounded`
- `cyclic`
- `infinite_candidate`
- `transfinite_candidate`
- `open`
- `unknown`

Hence:

$$
\\boxed{
\\mathsf{CoverageComplete}
\\not\\Rightarrow
\\mathsf{RegressTerminated}
}
$$

and:

$$
\\boxed{
\\mathsf{RegressTerminated}
\\not\\Rightarrow
\\mathsf{CoverageComplete}.
}
$$

## 1.4 First-Cause Sufficiency Test repair

The First-Cause Sufficiency certificate should not contain an implicit foundationalist thesis.

Recommended structure:

$$
\\mathsf{FCSCert}
=
\\left\\langle
\\mathsf{GenSuff},
\\mathsf{RespClosed},
\\mathsf{GroundCoverage},
\\mathsf{RegressStatus},
\\mathsf{PriorityMode},
\\mathsf{ExternalDebt},
\\mathsf{ClaimGate}
\\right\\rangle.
$$

No `RegressStatus` value automatically proves absolute first causality.

# 2. Agency, Intervention and Causal Power

## 2.1 Real-world action is not automatically a model intervention

Jørgensen, Gresele and Weichwald formalize the missing correspondence between actions in the world and mathematical interventions in a causal model. Their central circularity/impossibility result shows that a seemingly natural interpretation can trivialize interventional validity.

UCT should therefore add:

$$
\\boxed{
\\mathsf{Action}
\\neq
\\mathsf{Intervention}
\\neq
\\mathsf{CausalControl}.
}
$$

### Canonical repair

Introduce:

$$
\\mathsf{ActionInterventionCert}
\\left(
a,
do(X=x)
\\mid
\\Theta
\\right).
$$

Without this certificate, an available real-world action cannot serve as an intervention witness merely because the model contains a corresponding $do(\\cdot)$ expression.

## 2.2 Causal contribution is not strategic or epistemic control

Baier, Funke and Majumdar separate forward responsibility, strategic backward responsibility, and causal backward responsibility. Their examples show that causal responsibility can hold without the knowledge or strategy required for strategic responsibility.

UCT should distinguish:

$$
\\mathsf{CauseImpact},
\\quad
\\mathsf{StrategicControl},
\\quad
\\mathsf{EpistemicControl},
\\quad
\\mathsf{ProspectiveGuarantee},
\\quad
\\mathsf{RetrospectiveResponsibility}.
$$

## 2.3 Impact is not instrumental control

Carey et al. distinguish response incentives, instrumental control incentives and impact incentives. A variable may be affected by an agent while remaining a side effect rather than an instrumentally controlled objective.

Therefore:

$$
\\boxed{
\\mathsf{Impact}
\\not\\Rightarrow
\\mathsf{InstrumentalControl}.
}
$$

UCT should not infer intentional agency from transformation capability alone.

## 2.4 Agency is frame-dependent

Abel et al. argue that agency attribution depends on a reference frame containing choices about boundary, causal variables, goal-pursuit criterion, and meaningful adaptation criterion.

### Canonical repair

Introduce:

$$
\\mathsf{AgencyFrame}
=
\\left\\langle
D,
\\mathfrak B,
\\mathsf{CausalVars},
\\mathsf{GoalCriterion},
\\mathsf{AdaptCriterion},
\\mathsf{Observer},
T
\\right\\rangle.
$$

Then:

$$
\\boxed{
\\mathsf{AgencyRank}(A)
\\text{ without an AgencyFrame is ill-typed.}
}
$$

## 2.5 Meta-causal agency repair

 $\\mathsf{MC}_k$ should be read as rewrite capability relative to a declared law stack and agency frame.

Additional obligations should include:

1. action-to-intervention interpretation;
2. interventional validity;
3. policy/strategy availability;
4. epistemic access;
5. objective/utility relation when intentional control is claimed;
6. agency-frame declaration;
7. rewrite-law provenance.

# 3. Open Systems, Heterogeneous Composition and Institutions

## 3.1 Open-system composition is mature prior art

Structured and decorated cospans already provide rigorous interface-based composition of open systems under explicit categorical assumptions. Therefore UCT should not claim categorical open-system composition as a standalone invention.

## 3.2 Reachability can be strictly coarser than operational semantics

Open Petri-net theory provides a particularly important domain example. Operational semantics retains process structure. Reachability semantics collapses that structure to whether an output marking is reachable from an input marking.

For general composition, reachability is only lax and can miss behaviors involving repeated traversal across the shared interface.

This provides strong external evidence for:

$$
\\boxed{
\\mathsf{ReachabilitySemantics}
\\neq
\\mathsf{OperationalProcessSemantics}.
}
$$

## 3.3 Open-system bridges remain doctrine-relative

Myers' open dynamical systems and Libkind–Myers' double-operadic theory are highly general, but the specific doctrine, interaction theory, carrier and interfaces remain parameters.

Thus UCT's defensible role is:

$$
\\boxed{
\\text{meta-level protocol for declaring and auditing bridges}
}
$$

rather than a replacement for categorical systems theory.

## 3.4 Institution-level translation is high-threat prior art

Institution theory and heterogeneous specification already treat different logics as distinct systems connected by explicit morphisms/comorphisms. Such translations carry preservation obligations rather than being free identifications.

### Canonical repair

Replace minimal bridge metadata with:

$$
\\mathsf{BridgeDecl}^{X\\to Y}
=
\\left\\langle
\\mathsf{SourceType},
\\mathsf{TargetType},
\\mathsf{Doctrine},
\\mathsf{Interface},
\\mathsf{Translation},
\\mathsf{Preservation},
\\mathsf{Reflection},
\\mathsf{Coherence},
\\mathsf{Debt}
\\right\\rangle.
$$

Possible preservation obligations include satisfaction preservation, behavioural-equivalence preservation, reachability soundness/completeness, invariant preservation, provenance preservation, boundary compatibility, conservative extension, compositionality and multi-bridge coherence.

Hence:

$$
\\mathsf{BridgeExists}
\\not\\Rightarrow
\\mathsf{BridgeIsLossless},
$$

$$
\\mathsf{BridgeExists}
\\not\\Rightarrow
\\mathsf{BridgeIsConservative},
$$

and:

$$
\\mathsf{PairwiseBridge}
\\not\\Rightarrow
\\mathsf{GlobalCoherence}.
$$

# 4. Novelty Reclassification

## 4.1 Claims substantially absorbed by prior art

The following should no longer be presented as standalone UCT novelty:

1. grounding as non-causal metaphysical explanation;
2. distinguishing a causal first event from a foundational ground;
3. grounding-base / completeness accounts of fundamentality;
4. intervention-based causal semantics;
5. causal vs strategic vs epistemic responsibility;
6. frame-dependent agency in the broad sense;
7. interface-based categorical composition of open systems;
8. heterogeneous logical translations with preservation conditions.

## 4.2 Strongest remaining UCT contribution candidate

$$
\\mathsf{UCTContributionCandidate}
=
\\mathsf{TypedNonCollapse}
+
\\mathsf{EvidenceBearingBridgeDeclarations}
+
\\mathsf{BridgeNoGoAndCoherence}
+
\\mathsf{GroundingRegressSeparation}
+
\\mathsf{ActionInterventionInterpretation}
+
\\mathsf{CrossLayerDebtAccounting}.
$$

The candidate novelty is the **single audit architecture** in which typed promotion across these domains must carry explicit evidence, preservation conditions, obstruction states and outstanding debt.
