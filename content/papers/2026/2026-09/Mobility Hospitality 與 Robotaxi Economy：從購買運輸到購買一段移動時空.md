# **Mobility Hospitality（MH）**
## **移動款待／移動時空服務**

Mobility Hospitality 並不是把出租車改成豪華飯店，也不要求所有 robotaxi 都走高價路線。它描述的是一種服務結構轉換：

$$
\boxed{

\rightarrow
\text{Mobility Space Service}.
}

因此乘客購買的不再只是：

> 「用某種品質、某種隱私、某種車艙能力與某種活動模式，把我送到目的地。」

本文第一個核心命題是：

$$
\boxed{
\text{Fare}
\neq
\text{Price of Distance Only}.
}
$$

現實 ride-hailing 本來就會依距離、時間、供需、機場、等待與車型調整價格。2026 年 robotaxi 市場更開始出現服務分層：Waymo 在 2026 年推出 Premier 月費會員，提供 priority pickup、ride savings、early access 與 cancellation flexibility；Zoox 在 2026 年 8 月開始 Las Vegas 付費服務時，Reuters 報導其 fare structure 對標傳統 ride-hailing 的 comfort tier，約比 standard fare 高一個 premium 範圍；而 2026 年 Bay Area 的第三方價格研究顯示 Waymo 相對 Uber / Lyft 的平均價格 premium 正在收窄。這些現象並不證明未來一定採用本文的 PMS 分級，但已顯示 autonomous ride-hailing 不必只用「最低價格」作為競爭維度。

$$

# Abstract

Once mobility systems can provide programmable cabin environments, persistent digital continuity, zone-aware interfaces, AI agents, privacy, work, rest, entertainment, and social modes, the economic product is no longer adequately described as transportation alone.

This paper introduces Mobility Hospitality (MH), a service framework in which the journey itself becomes a designed and differentiable product. The basic ride contract expands from origin, destination, time, fare, and safety to include cabin space, privacy, comfort, activity support, continuity, personalization, and service quality.

Current 2026 market developments provide early evidence of service segmentation. Waymo has introduced a paid Premier membership with priority and loyalty benefits, while Zoox has begun paid Las Vegas rides with pricing positioned around the comfort tier of conventional ride-hailing. Robotaxi and automated-taxi research also shows that hedonic motivation, price value, in-vehicle features, trust, personalization, and privacy influence adoption and choice.

The paper defines mobility service tiers, cabin capability classes, experience service-level agreements, journey contracts, fleet matching, membership models, and a multi-component profit function. It explicitly distinguishes hospitality from luxury: a high-quality basic mobility experience may consist of reliability, cleanliness, quiet, connectivity, accessibility, and predictable privacy without expensive entertainment hardware.

The paper also treats privacy as both a governance constraint and a potential service attribute. Personalization must not become unlimited surveillance, and shared vehicles require strict teardown and identity separation.

The resulting framework turns cabin capability into a market attribute and establishes the economic layer needed for B-07's unified Programmable Mobility Continuum reference architecture.

# 225. 結論

出租車的歷史產品承諾非常簡單：

> 給錢，我把你送到目的地。

這個承諾不會消失。

但當 robotaxi、AI、多螢幕、Spatial Audio、網路、可程式環境與數位 Continuity 逐漸成熟後，服務可以多出第二層：

> **你希望怎麼度過這段旅程？**

這個問題會把市場從：

$$
\boxed{
\text{Price}
+
\text{ETA}
}
$$

逐步擴張成：

$$
\boxed{
\text{Price}
+
\text{ETA}
+
\text{Journey Quality}
+
\text{Activity Fit}
+
\text{Privacy}
+
\text{Continuity}.
}
$$

2026 年的市場已經看到非常早期但清楚的分層訊號：Waymo 開始賣 membership / priority；Zoox 開始以 comfort-tier pricing 進入付費服務；purpose-built robotaxi 把 in-cabin experience 當成產品本體；robotaxi adoption 研究也開始把 hedonic value、in-vehicle features、price value、personalization 與 privacy 放進 choice model。

因此未來「豪華出租車」不一定只代表：

> 車牌比較貴、座椅是真皮。

它可能代表：

> **一段具有可驗證空間能力、可程式模式、資料主權、AI Continuity 與服務保證的移動時空。**

而最低階的優質 Mobility Hospitality 也不一定昂貴。

它甚至可能只是：

> 乾淨、安靜、準時、不要打擾我、讓我有網路、讓我安全下車、不要留下我的資料。

這些其實就是 Hospitality。

當 transportation infrastructure 變得更加自動化之後，真正的差異化可能逐步從：

$$
\text{Who drives?}
$$

轉向：

$$
\boxed{
\text{What kind of time and space do you get while moving?}
}
$$

B-07 將把這個服務經濟層與前五篇技術層正式合併，形成完整 Programmable Mobility Continuum Reference Architecture。
