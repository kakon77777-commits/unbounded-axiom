# Sources

## Primary mathematical sources

1. Timothy S. Trudgian, “An improved upper bound for the argument of the
   Riemann zeta-function on the critical line II,” *Journal of Number
   Theory* 134 (2014), 280–292.
   DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2013.07.017>

   本節點使用 published abstract 所列

   $$
   |S(T)|\le0.112\log T+0.278\log\log T+2.510,
   \qquad T\ge e.
   $$

   arXiv record：<https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.5846>。其 abstract 顯示
   較小的 preprint constants；本節點沒有使用那組數字。

2. A. P. Guinand, “A Summation Formula in the Theory of Prime Numbers,”
   *Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society* s2-50 (1948),
   107–119. DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s2-50.2.107>

   只作 explicit-formula 歷史脈絡。v0.4 並未宣稱已把本有限矩陣模型
   完整 interval-transfer 到 Guinand–Weil 顯式公式。
