# Exact symmetric-family search method

For a rotated polygonal curve with vertices $(x_i(\phi),y_i(\phi))$, the exact
homothetic scale of the certified Wetzel triangle is

$$
s(\phi)=
\max_i\left(\frac{x_i(\phi)}A+\frac{y_i(\phi)}B\right)
-\frac{\min_i x_i(\phi)}A
-\frac{\min_i y_i(\phi)}B.
$$

The outer objective maximizes $\min_\phi s(\phi)$ over mirror-symmetric
five-link and seven-link parameterizations. Reflection is already included:
the curve equals its mirror up to parameter reversal.
