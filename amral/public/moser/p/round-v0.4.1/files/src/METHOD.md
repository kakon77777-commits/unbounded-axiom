# Branch equalization method

For the mirror-symmetric five-link family, four weak local minima of the phase
landscape are tracked. The epigraph problem is

$$
\max_{\eta,z} z
$$

subject to

$$
m_r(\eta)\ge z,\qquad r=1,\ldots,4.
$$

At the numerical max-min point, a second-level branch pressure vector
$\mu_r\ge0$, $\sum_r\mu_r=1$ is computed so that

$$
\sum_r\mu_r\nabla_\eta m_r\approx0.
$$
