# Round 6 method

The general five-link neighborhood is parameterized by four symmetric variables
and four antisymmetric variables. The objective is

$$
s_{\mathrm{cong}}(\gamma)
=
\min\{s_+(\gamma),s_-(\gamma)\},
$$

where each branch minimizes the exact three-facet Wetzel scale over rotation.

A local stability draft samples symmetric parameter boxes around the round-5
event root and checks whether all four prescribed contact signatures remain
unchanged. This is not an interval proof.
